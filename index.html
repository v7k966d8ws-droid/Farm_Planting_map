Jacks Planting Map — Prototype

Tap planting sections to mark the area planted. One bay = 6 rows + tractor track + 6 rows.

For tapping/saving, open this page through Safari/GitHub Pages. ChatGPT’s file preview can show the map but does not run the app.

Prototype interaction: each visible row strip is divided into 6 sections along its length. Tap/drag your finger over sections. A normal 36-row pass is 3 adjacent bays.
